# Editorial policy

Positive journalism must remain rigorous journalism. We cover progress and constructive developments without hiding setbacks, limitations or meaningful context.

## Editorial mix
Initial planning guide: 60% positive global stories, 30% specialist international news and 10% important wider developments. These are guideposts, not quotas. Do not publish weak or irrelevant material to meet a percentage.

## Human approval
AI may assist with discovery, clustering, research organisation and first-draft preparation. No article may be published without explicit human editorial approval.

## Verification
Before approval, check names, dates, places, figures, primary sources, independent corroboration, quote context, headline accuracy, image ownership/licensing and whether the reporting is genuinely constructive rather than misleadingly framed.

## Corrections and transparency
Keep records of article versions, sources, approvals and material edits. Correct errors promptly and visibly. A material edit after approval requires another approval.

## Copyright and source use
Use third-party sources to discover and verify stories, not reproduce their articles. Respect copyright, terms of service, attribution requirements and feed/API conditions. RSS availability does not automatically grant republication rights.
