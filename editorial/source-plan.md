# Initial source plan

Target: 30–50 carefully selected sources, prioritising original and authoritative material.

## Proposed coverage mix
- 8–10 international newsrooms and wire services for discovery and corroboration.
- 6–8 science, research and university sources.
- 5–7 environment, conservation and wildlife organisations.
- 4–6 aviation and aerospace sources.
- 3–5 food, coffee, cacao and agriculture sources.
- 3–5 sports federations and governing bodies.
- 3–5 technology and innovation sources.
- 2–4 culture, travel and tourism sources.

## Source onboarding checklist
Record organisation, source URL, desk/category, source type, geographic coverage, RSS/API availability, terms of use, rate limits, attribution rules, image permissions, reliability notes and last checked date.

Prefer primary sources for announcements, research and statistics. Use independent reporting to corroborate claims and add context. Do not scrape sites where terms prohibit it. Do not assume RSS or API access permits republication. Produce original reporting and summaries with clear attribution.
