# Brand guidelines

## Positioning
A global digital publication focused on constructive, uplifting stories and specialist international reporting. Positive, but not naïve. Evidence-led, curious and globally minded.

## Visual identity
- Forest green: #183D32
- Deep green: #102B24
- Cream: #F3F0E7
- Paper: #FAF8F1
- Muted gold: #B69A5D
- Display type: Playfair Display
- Supporting type: DM Sans

## Voice
Clear, intelligent, warm and curious. Avoid hollow optimism, clickbait, unverified claims and forced positivity.

## Working tagline
Good news. Wider perspective.

## Status
Working identity only. Name and domain require clearance before public launch.
